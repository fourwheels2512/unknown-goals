# Claims we make, claims we do not make

One page for a reader or a reviewer who wants the scope without
the paper. Every row in the first table points at the artifact or command
that backs it; every row in the second names what would earn the claim.
Numbers are as of the artifact set of 2026-09-12 (through the fourth phone
sitting and the leave-one-out mechanism ablation); the paper and
`docs/EMBODIED.md` carry the full protocols and caveats.

## Claims we do not make (and what would earn them)

| we do not claim | why not | what would earn it |
|---|---|---|
| Any result on a **physical robot** (Boston Dynamics Spot, TurtleBot, a hospital cart, anything). | Every embodied number is from a simulator or, for the real-pixels battery, from a hand-held camera carried by the owner through one house; locomotion is teleport-abstracted in THOR/SubT/hospital and a simulated diff-drive under Gazebo/ROS 2. | The packaged ROS 2 stack driven on hardware with its own perception, graded by the same typed client, numbers reported with the same caveat discipline. |
| Any **embedded / edge-board** figure (NVIDIA Jetson, Raspberry Pi, any ARM SoC). | The edge profile is a laptop CPU (i9-11900H) on Windows 11 and WSL 2; nothing else was measured. | `experiments/edge_profile.py` run on the board, artifact committed under `data/artifacts/edge/`. |
| Any **clinical, hospital-operations, or patient-safety** claim, or any regulatory status (ISO 13482, IEC 62304, FDA). | The hospital result is a fetch benchmark on a public simulated world; the standards are cited as the properties they ask for, not as certifications held. | A deployment study on a real fleet with the standard's evidence package; none is planned by the author. |
| **SLAM, mapping, control, grasp planning, or learned perception** of our own. | Sensing reads simulator state (THOR oracle, logical camera, scene graph) or a third-party open-vocabulary detector on rendered frames or on a hand-held camera's frames; in the Nav2 variant navigation is Nav2's and the map is SLAM Toolbox's, while the sensor vantage is still the physics pose; grasping is a `grasp_pose` hand-off, MoveIt 2 not run. | Running those stacks and grading them; they are named as the variable held constant, not the variable measured. |
| **Domain-general superiority over LLMs**, or a fair comparison against the strongest agentic scaffolds. | Home-field benchmark; our scaffolds bound the LLMs; frontier models already match accuracy. The claim is structural (latency, cost, privacy, determinism, proofs). | Independent replication with stronger scaffolds on a benchmark neither side designed. |
| **Natural-language understanding** of any kind. | Ingestion is structured claims over a closed ontology; NL is a designed seam (`CognitiveProposer`) with no model behind it in every evaluated path. Text benchmarks that need NL understanding (HotpotQA, MuSiQue, LongMemEval) were declared not runnable engine-only rather than run with outside help. | An LLM proposer in hybrid mode, reported as hybrid, never in the LLM-free tables. |
| Habitat, OmniGibson, or Isaac results. | Habitat is blocked on WSL 2's EGL; OmniGibson is installed but not run. Reported as feasibility, not results. | A native-Linux or cloud-GPU run of the same adapter pattern. |
| **Statistical significance** beyond binomial intervals, or multi-machine variance. | Single deterministic runs; Wilson intervals in captions; world-sampling variance across seed families unquantified. | Seed-family replication on other hardware (the author's decision was that 8 seeds suffice for this release). |
| **Driving the Mars rover or the underwater vehicle, sonar perception, Space ROS itself, or lunar worlds.** | The field batteries move a station cursor over physics state with a logical mast sensor and a declared sonar curve; the demo packages were built on vanilla ROS 2 Jazzy; HoloOcean is blocked on WSL2. | Stage-C drive loops (the demo's rover controllers; ArduSub SITL + MAVROS for DAVE), a real sonar model, a Space ROS container run, an OmniLRS or Lunar Autonomy Challenge world. |
| A **flat latency curve** over an unbounded lifetime. | Growth is cut ~7×, then a further 4×, then about 3×, but per-day latency still grows 4.5–8.7× first-to-last window on a 300-day soak. | The remaining incremental-view-maintenance increments (an ordered contradicted-claim index, an event-time-sorted per-subject index) and/or bounded-memory consolidation, re-measured on the same soak. |
| A **justified NOT-FOUND verdict**: that the engine recognises an absent object and refuses with a proof of exhaustive search. | `GoalStatus` has no refusal state and a null verdict spans "no verdict" and "budget exhausted"; at a raised resolve bar the exhaustion rule names a room for an absent object in 121/300 episodes (PAPER §4.6). | A refusal state, a null verdict disambiguated from exhaustion, and a proof of exhaustiveness over the rooms rather than over the hypothesis beam, scored 0/300 wrong on the absent arm of `experiments/absent_target.py` at bar 0.9. |
| Detection of **thin or tiny objects** by the real-perception front-end. | Forks and keychains in garbage cans and flat credit cards sit below YOLO-World's recall at 1080²; those episodes are reported as misses with the probe evidence, never as wrong commitments (structurally impossible: absence of a detection cannot create a sighting). | A better detector or a closer-look policy; either would be reported the same way. |
| That the belief arithmetic is finished. | Under the shipped treatment, two very old sightings lose their recency ordering; a measured ordering-preserving variant exists default-off (0 of 360 re-ranks, `data/artifacts/staleness_ordering.json`). | Adopting it as default is a config-version bump and a re-baseline of the battery, calibration, and baseline artifacts. |
| That this is a **product with a company behind it**. | It is one author's engine and paper: independent research with no team, funding, customers, or company involvement (the author's unrelated business had no role in the work; PAPER, funding and competing interests). The benchmark kit (generator, baselines, LLM harness, proof checker, every trace and artifact) is public under Apache-2.0; the engine's source is withheld, and a demonstration build is released with the kit. | — |
